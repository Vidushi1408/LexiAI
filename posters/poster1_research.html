01

Objective

Build a private, auditable platform that turns contracts, reports and SOPs into executive briefings, compliance verdicts, extracted clauses and answers that cite their source — without sending documents to a third-party cloud — then measure honestly how well its retrieval, refusal and classification components work.

02

Hypothesis

H1

Hybrid search beats keyword-only search

Blending BM25 keyword scores with dense-vector similarity finds the gold passage more often than BM25 alone.

Supported
H2

A similarity threshold enables honest refusals

A relevance cut-off lets the system answer “not found” on unanswerable questions instead of guessing.

Supported · needs tuning
H3

A small neural network can tag contract sentences

On 384-d sentence embeddings, a compact ANN, CNN or LSTM labels 7 sentence types far above the 14% chance level.

Supported · ANN best
03

Procedure

1

Ingest & guard

PDF · DOCX · XLSX · PPTX · CSV · EML · audio → text with true page provenance; upload validation and a prompt-injection scan.

2

Pre-process

Clean, tokenise, remove stop-words, lemmatise (NLTK10).

3

Index

3-sentence overlapping chunks → MiniLM3,4 384-d embeddings → FAISS2 vector index + BM251 keyword index.

4

Retrieve

Blend both scores; return the top-5 passages with document, page and relevance.

5

Reason (RAG)5

A local Llama 3.2 3B9 answers only from retrieved passages with numbered citations; below the relevance threshold it refuses.

6

Analyse

Compliance checklist · BERT NER6,7 + clause rules · ANN / CNN / LSTM8 tagger · briefings · action items.

7

Govern

Role-based access, hash-chained audit log, optional zero-retention mode.

Retrieval & refusal test

5 sector sets (consulting, finance, healthcare, HR/ops, legal): 63 answerable + 22 unanswerable questions. Gold = right document + page + text.

Tagger test

280 hand-written sentences, 7 tags × 40. Split: 218 train · 31 val · 31 test. 80 epochs, Adam.

05

Conclusion

  • Hybrid retrieval is a clear win — better Recall@1 than keyword-only in 3 of 5 sectors, never worse on any measure; +7.9 pts overall.
  • Vector similarity makes honest refusals possible; BM25 scores alone cannot separate answerable from unanswerable questions.
  • A simple ANN is the best tagger here; the LSTM adds cost without benefit.
  • Limits: small evaluation sets; thresholds tuned on the same data (optimistic); a 31-sentence test set; and a cautious 3B local LLM — across eight live compliance runs on a 10-page contract it passed 0–4 of 6 checks (about 2 on average) and routed the rest to human review; a verified quote proves a passage exists, not that it proves the point.
  • Next: blind, larger evaluation · SSO · multi-tenancy · hosted deployment and monitoring.
